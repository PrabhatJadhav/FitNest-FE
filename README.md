📖 Fitness App
A modern fitness application that promotes gym routines, healthy eating, and progress tracking.

🚀 Features

- [ ] Workout Management – Custom & pre-made workout plans
- [ ] Exercise Library – Detailed exercise guides by muscle group & equipment
- [ ] Nutrition & Recipes – Meal planning, calorie tracking, and healthy recipes
- [ ] Progress & Analytics – Workout history, body measurements, and achievements
- [ ] Community & Challenges – Fitness challenges, leaderboards, and social interactions
- [ ] Personalized Dashboard – Overview of user progress

🛠 Tech Stack
✅ `Frontend: AngularJS`
✅ `Backend: Node.js with Express`
✅ `Database: PostgreSQL`
✅ `Authentication: JWT-based authentication`
✅ `Styling: TailwindCSS`
